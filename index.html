Mango wood is the base material across nearly the whole catalog, so it isn't a useful split on its own. Three separate things happening in one block. The key highlights bullets hold attention (~6s), but the small info icon beside them gets almost no clicks, it needs to be visibly bigger and more clearly clickable, a fix already queued for build. The "More" offers toggle is being actively clicked open, worth testing it defaulted open rather than collapsed. NextFour moves across this block. Enlarge the key highlights slightly so they carry more weight. Make the info icon larger and more clearly interactive, and have it open the pricing and savings detail inline when tapped, rather than sending visitors scrolling down the page to find it. Set the "More" offers toggle to open by default, visitors are already clicking to expand it. And rework Pay in 3, engagement with the accordion is low, so it's worth rethinking how instalment options are presented rather than keeping the current format.

03Not working

Sticky Add to Basket bar

GA4 clicks, both devices combined17 total

Interaction here is low. This bar is sticky, visible for the entire scroll on every device, so reach isn't the constraint, people simply aren't clicking it. It's also consistent with what we saw in earlier Clarity data on this same sticky bar, low to no clicks there as well, so this isn't a one-off reading. Next step is a sanity push on the button itself, a design or placement change, to see if that moves the number at all. Worth also testing it as an on/off toggle, run the page with the bar and without it, and see whether its absence costs anything or whether it's just dead weight.

NextPush the redesigned version of the button first, then run an A/B test with the sticky bar present versus removed entirely, to confirm whether it earns its place or is simply going unused.

07

Next steps

The plan in three parts: the immediate work to do first, the two strategic directions open to us after that, and then the specific next step for each section of the page.

Start here, before anything else

Sanity checks to push now

  • Redesign the Add to Basket button, main and sticky
  • Enlarge the key highlights and their info icon
  • Slightly enlarge the price and savings icon
  • Swap red accents to green, a clearer signal by colour psychology

Tracking to add

  • GA4 tracking on the sticky section nav, to confirm the ranking we currently only have from Clarity
  • Swipe-depth tracking on The Details, to see how far through the carousel visitors get
  • Page load speed, to track and improve over time

Two ways forward, once the above is done

Option A, deepen the cabinet investigation

  • Our early analysis points to specific cabinets, and specific materials, underperforming
  • Deep dive into those, and work through whether we can lift them before scaling

Option B, roll out to the full catalog

  • Make the sanity changes above, then take the template live across the rest of the storage range

Section by section, what happens next

Gallery and lightbox

  • Add supporting captions to the thumbnails and test the effect
  • Add pinch-and-tap zoom, seen attempted in session recordings
  • Desktop, redesign to thumbnails-left so it isn't a full-height scroll

Key Highlights, Offers & Pay in 3

  • Enlarge highlights and the info icon, open pricing detail inline on tap
  • Default the offers toggle open
  • Rework how Pay in 3 is presented, engagement is low

Sticky Add to Basket bar

  • Push the redesigned button, then A/B test with the bar versus without it

Sticky section nav

  • Add GA4 tracking to confirm the hierarchy
  • Test a shorter nav, fewer links, more prominent
